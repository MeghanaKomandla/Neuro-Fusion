Dementia Speech Biomarkers
🎤

Pause Ratio

AD patients exhibit significantly higher inter-word and inter-phrase pause rates (König et al., 2015)

📈

MFCC Variance

Reduced phoneme diversity and articulation precision captured via spectral cepstral coefficients

🔊

Energy Dynamics

Flattened vocal energy and monotone prosody are consistent early-stage AD markers (Luz, 2020)

🎼

Pitch Monotonicity

Dementia speech becomes flatter and less prosodically varied over time
